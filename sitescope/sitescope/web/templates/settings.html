Responsible use
SiteScope performs a passive assessment: it requests pages the way a browser does and analyses the responses. It does not attempt to exploit weaknesses, submit data or access accounts. Even so, only scan a site you own or have written permission to test.
A clear result means no problems were found in the areas SiteScope checks. It is not a guarantee that a website is secure. Sites handling payments or sensitive personal information should also be assessed by a qualified security professional.
